# B.E.E.P. Reference-Home Approval Governance Policy

**Status:** DRAFT — Pending B.E.E.P. Board Adoption  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Purpose

Establish a consistent, transparent, and documented process for reviewing, approving, rejecting, and retiring reference homes used in the B.E.E.P. Software Consistency Repository.

## 2. Final Approval Authority

The B.E.E.P. Board of Directors retains final authority over all reference-home approval, rejection, and retirement decisions.

No individual technical reviewer, repository administrator, software vendor, or outside organization may independently authorize a reference home for official testing.

## 3. Technical Review

Qualified technical reviewers may be appointed or authorized by the Board.

Reviewers shall evaluate:

- Reference-home specifications and supporting documentation.
- Building geometry, envelope assemblies, and mechanical systems.
- Energy modeling inputs and assumptions.
- Applicable compliance pathways and calculation requirements.
- Reproducibility and consistency of reference-home data.
- Identified discrepancies and corrective actions.

Reviewers shall provide written findings and recommendations to the Board.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

Reviewers and Board members participating in approval decisions shall disclose relevant financial, professional, or organizational conflicts of interest.

The Board shall document how identified conflicts are managed, including any required recusal.

Software vendors may provide technical clarification but shall not approve their own reference-home submissions.

## 5. Approval Process

Reference homes shall follow this process:

1. DEVELOPMENT — Reference-home specifications are prepared and internally validated.
2. UNDER_REVIEW — Authorized technical reviewers evaluate the reference home.
3. BOARD DECISION — The Board considers the technical findings and supporting evidence.
4. APPROVED — The Board formally authorizes the reference home for official testing.
5. RETIRED — The Board formally removes a previously approved reference-home version from active use.

A rejected proposal shall remain unapproved and may be revised and resubmitted.

## 6. Required Approval Documentation

Each Board decision shall be supported by:

- Reference-home ID and version.
- Technical review findings and recommendation.
- Relevant supporting evidence.
- Conflict-of-interest disclosures and disposition.
- Board decision and decision date.
- Board authorization reference, such as approved meeting minutes or written resolution.
- Repository pull request or change reference.

## 7. Repository Controls

The repository shall maintain automated validation of reference-home metadata, approval records, and audit history.

Approval information must be consistent across the reference-home registry, approval documentation, and audit log.

Historical audit records shall remain append-only.

A successful automated validation does not substitute for formal Board authorization.

## 8. Changes and Retirement

Material changes to an approved reference home shall require a new version and appropriate technical review and Board authorization before release.

Retirement decisions shall be documented without deleting historical approval records.

## 9. Policy Adoption

This policy becomes effective only upon formal adoption by the B.E.E.P. Board of Directors.
